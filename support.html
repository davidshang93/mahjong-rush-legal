About the game

Mahjong Rush 3D Match Dim Sum is a single-player 3D tile-matching game set in a cozy Chinatown dim sum house. In Dim Sum Rush, mahjong tiles tumble into a bamboo steamer or a big wok: tap any tile you can see to move it into the tray, and match three of the same picture to clear them. In 3D Puzzles, tiles are stacked into 3D shapes such as lanterns, pandas and pagodas: turn the shape to find free tiles, clear it, and unlock the treasure inside.

How to play

  • Tap a tile to move it into the tray at the bottom. Three tiles with the same picture clear automatically.
  • The tray has 7 slots. If it fills up, the level is lost.
  • 3D Puzzles: drag with one finger to turn the shape, pinch with two fingers to zoom, and tap the round button to straighten it again. Darker tiles are covered by other tiles and can’t be tapped yet.
  • Boosters at the bottom: Undo puts the last tile back; Move Out takes three tiles out of the tray; Shake (Dim Sum Rush) tosses the tiles; Shuffle and Magnet help in 3D Puzzles.
  • Pause: tap the pause button at the top left. Sound can be switched there or on the home screen.

Questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically, on your device only. There is no account and no cloud save, so progress does not move between devices.

How do I reset my progress? Delete the App and install it again. This erases all coins, stars and treasures and cannot be undone.

Is every level solvable? Yes. 3D Puzzles are generated so that they can always be cleared, even after a shuffle. In Dim Sum Rush every visible tile can be tapped, so it comes down to managing the tray.

The game runs slowly or gets warm. Close other apps, and make sure Low Power Mode is off.

Are there ads? Only optional rewarded videos: you can watch one to get one more use of a booster, to revive once in a level, or to double the coins from a level. Nothing plays unless you tap to watch.

How do I report an inappropriate ad? While the ad is playing, tap its info (i) or AdChoices icon to report it to Google. You can also email contact@eastbigtech.com with a short description of the ad and, if you can, a screenshot.

Does the App collect my data? Your game progress stays on your device and we never receive it. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device and ad information; if you allow tracking it may also use your advertising identifier. You can change tracking in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
